Describe your situation

VANTAM provides practical relocation, application and arrival support. We do not replace official institutions, licensed legal advisors, immigration consultants or rental agencies. For formal legal, tax or immigration matters, we'll help route you to a qualified professional.

How it works

Send the situation. We take it from there.

No long intake forms. No bureaucratic back-and-forth. A clear, practical response within 24 hours.

01

You send the situation

Briefly describe what needs to be arranged — housing, documents, arrival, settling in. Location, timing, and any details you have.

02

We review and define the task

We identify the service category, timeline and route. You receive a clear response with a recommended next step — usually within 24 hours.

03

We coordinate the support

We organise the right support, prepare documents, contact agencies, and manage the moving parts so you don't have to chase anyone.

04

You stay informed

One point of contact. Practical communication. Parents can be kept in the loop on request. You always know what's happening and what's next.

Why VANTAM

The gap between arriving and being settled is expensive. We close it.

International students arrive in the Netherlands and lose weeks to disorganised logistics — confusing rental applications, missed registration windows, unclear landlord communication, and services that don't connect to each other.

VANTAM coordinates the practical side. Not a relocation agency, not a moving company, not a licensed legal advisor — we're the coordination layer that connects them, calmly and locally.

One contact, not ten

You don't manage multiple providers, agencies and platforms. We coordinate them.

Practical, not theoretical

No generic advice. We respond with next steps, not information you could already Google.

Local presence

Based in The Hague, operating across the Randstad. We know the cities, the agencies, and the pitfalls.

Scam awareness built in

Honest boundaries
