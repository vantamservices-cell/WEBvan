Why VANTAM

The gap between arriving and being settled is expensive. We close it.

Students, expats and newcomers often lose weeks to disorganised logistics — wrong furniture delivery dates, missed registration windows, unclear landlord communication, and services that don't connect.

VANTAM coordinates the practical side. Not a relocation agency, not a moving company, not a housing agent — something more useful: someone who handles the coordination between all of them.
